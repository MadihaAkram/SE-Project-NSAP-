NSAP — NAMAL Student Assistance Provider

Project Summary:
NSAP (NAMAL Student Assistance Provider) is a proposed web-based system designed to improve how the Student Support Office (SSO) manages student records and support-related information at Namal University.
The system aims to replace scattered or manual record-handling methods with a centralized platform where students can submit their information and required documents, while authorized SSO staff can review and manage these records efficiently.

Key Features:
Student Portal: Allows students to enter and submit their required information and documents.
SSO Staff Portal: Enables authorized staff to access and manage student records.
Organized Student Records: Organizes records by academic year, department, and student name or registration number.
Document Review: Allows SSO staff to review submitted documents and notify students about missing information.
Announcements: Enables SSO staff to share important announcements with students.
Change Requests: Allows students to request changes to approved information, subject to SSO staff review and approval.
Privacy and Access Control: Students can access their own records, while authorized SSO staff can manage the relevant student information.

Tech Stack:
The planned technologies for this project include:
HTML5: For structuring the web pages.
CSS3: For styling and designing the user interface.
JavaScript: For interactive features and client-side functionality.
Bootstrap: For responsive layouts and reusable UI components.
Figma: For UI/UX design and prototyping.
